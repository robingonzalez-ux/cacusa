Size Guide

by Taitus

So your jewelry fits perfectly, whether you're buying it for yourself or gifting it. Here's how our adjustable rings work, how to measure your exact ring size at home, and how to choose the right necklace or chain length.

Adjustable rings

Most of our rings are adjustable: they have a small opening in the band that you can gently open or close with your fingers to fit a wide range of sizes (roughly US 5 to 9). They're the safest option if you don't know your exact size, or if you're gifting without being able to measure the other person.

Tip: adjust the ring gently and gradually — opening or closing the band too fast can weaken the metal over time.

Fixed-size rings — how to measure your size at home

Some of our pieces come in one specific size (not adjustable). You can work out your size with either of these two methods, using just a ruler:

  1. String method: wrap a piece of string or a strip of paper around the base of the finger you'll wear the ring on, mark where it overlaps, and measure that length in millimeters with a ruler. That number is your circumference — compare it to the table below.
  2. Ring-that-already-fits method: take a ring you already wear on that same finger and measure its inner diameter (edge to edge, through the center) in millimeters. Compare it to the diameter column in the table.
